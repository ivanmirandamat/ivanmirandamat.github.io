Ivan Miranda

I am a Ph.D. student in Mathematics at IMPA, under the supervision of Lucas Ambrozio.

I am currently seeking postdoctoral positions. I expect to defend my Ph.D. before July 2027.

Research interests: I work in Differential Geometry and Geometric Analysis. My research includes stability and index questions for constant mean curvature hypersurfaces, motivated in part by a question of do Carmo, and min-max methods relating widths of embedded circles to the geometry of the ambient space. I am also interested in related problems in geometric analysis.
